Wire Progression

The general 24-month nonextraction sequence, from initial bonding to finishing wire.

Ask this at every adjustment

What objective is still unfinished, and what wire or mechanic best accomplishes it? This is a pathway driven by objectives, not a rigid list of wires. The wire is the answer to a question, not the question itself.

Scope

General comprehensive nonextraction treatment only. Extraction cases require a separate protocol, because bonding setup, segmental wiring, extraction timing, and space-closure mechanics all differ. Do not apply this sequence automatically to an extraction case.

Timing benchmark

Aim to reach the finishing-wire phase at roughly the halfway point of treatment. For a 24-month case that is around month 12 to 13. For an 18-month case, around month 9. If you are past halfway and not near stainless steel, raise it.

Before the stepsHow to read a case

The five overlapping phases

PhasePrimary objective
1. AlignmentInitial leveling and rotational correction with round NiTi.
2. LevelingCurve-of-Spee and deep-overbite correction using reverse-curve NiTi, and later step-down bends in stainless steel.
3. Space closureAnterior spaces first; posterior spaces later, when stronger torque control is available.
4. Bite correctionVertical, sagittal, midline, and occlusal corrections that may overlap the other phases.
5. FinishingTorque control, posterior space closure, detailing, settling, and final wire adjustments.

They overlap. A case can be finishing its alignment while starting its leveling.

Appointment assessment order

  1. Check treatment month and overall pace.
  2. Check alignment, rotations, and complete bracket engagement.
  3. Check anterior spaces and whether closure is being maintained.
  4. Check posterior spaces, but do not require them to be closed during the NiTi stages.
  5. Check deep overbite and Curve of Spee.
  6. Check the 7s and overall wire engagement.
  7. Check loose brackets, poking wires, oral hygiene, and anything else that may delay progression.
  8. Select the next wire or mechanic, and document the clinical reason.

Core clinical rules

RuleAO guideline
Starting wire14 NT is the routine start. 12 NT is reserved for unusually severe rotations.
Round NiTi cinching14 NT, 16 NT, and 18 NT are cinched at the terminal teeth.
18 NT is optionalUse it when rotations remain or the timeline allows. Skip it when alignment is adequate and the case should progress efficiently.
Anterior spacing in NiTiUse an anterior 3–3 lace to hold closure or prevent spaces opening. Do not rely on early NiTi power chain for active closure.
Anterior space closureClose meaningful anterior spaces on stainless steel, most commonly 16x22 SS, using 2–2 or 3–3 power chain.
Canine steel tiesWith 3–3 power chain, steel ties may be placed over the canines, under the power chain and over the archwire.
Posterior space closurePosterior spaces are intentionally allowed to remain through the NiTi sequence, and are usually addressed later in stainless steel, especially 19x25 SS, for better torque control.
21x25 NT power chainMay be used all around, but expect only limited closure — the wire is thick and NiTi friction is high.
Finishing wire19x25 SS is the usual finishing wire. Some underbite cases finish in 18x25 SS. 21x25 SS is rare, reserved for additional torque.

Deep overbite and reverse-curve NiTi

The rule that gets broken

Do not place step bends in NiTi. For a deep overbite or pronounced lower Curve of Spee, substitute the corresponding lower reverse-curve NiTi wire for the standard lower NiTi wire, with the anterior portion directed down. Once you are in stainless steel, step-down bends are allowed.

The nine stepsStep by step

Step 01

Initial Bonding

14 NT
Key reminderRound NiTi wires (14 NT, 16 NT, 18 NT) are cinched at the terminal teeth.
Written detail

Assess

  • Severity of rotations, to choose 14 NT versus rare 12 NT.
  • Anterior spacing at the time of bonding.
  • Bracket placement and that planned teeth are bonded for a nonextraction case.
  • Whether posterior engagement is reasonable for the starting wire.
  • Oral hygiene and immediate bracket or wire concerns.

Do

  • Place 14 NT in most cases; 12 NT only for severe rotations.
  • Cinch both ends of the round NiTi at the terminal teeth.
  • If anterior spacing is present, place an anterior 3–3 lace to prevent opening and possibly gain minor closure.
  • Steel ties on rotated teeth only when complete engagement can be achieved.
  • Prepare the arch for progression to 16 NT.

Document

  • Starting wire and any alternate 12 NT use.
  • Rotations and spacing.
  • Any 3–3 lace or steel ties placed.
  • Bracket or wire issues and the next-wire plan.
Back to flowchart
Step 02

First Adjustment

14 NT → 16 NT
Key reminderThe 16 NT should increasingly engage the 7s while maintaining anterior space control.
Written detail

Assess

  • Treatment timing and overall alignment progress.
  • That the 14 NT has expressed enough alignment to progress.
  • Anterior and posterior spaces.
  • Remaining rotations and incomplete bracket engagement.
  • Loose brackets, poking wires, oral hygiene.

Do

  • Place 16 NT.
  • Engage the 7s when possible.
  • Cinch distal ends at the terminal teeth.
  • Place or continue a 3–3 lace if anterior spaces are present.
  • Steel ties on rotated teeth when full engagement is needed.
  • Verify complete wire seating.

Document

  • 14 NT → 16 NT.
  • Spacing, rotations, steel ties, and lace.
  • Complete treatment card and clinical notes.
Back to flowchart
Step 03

Rotation / Timing Decision

16 NT → 18 NT or 16x22 NT
Key reminder18 NT is an optional alignment wire, not a mandatory step.
Written detail

Assess

  • Residual rotations, carefully.
  • Whether the 16 NT is fully engaged and the arch is sufficiently aligned.
  • Treatment timing. In a 24-month case, 18 NT is commonly used when rotations remain. In an 18-month-or-less case with no rotations, it may be skipped.
  • Anterior spacing, maintained with a 3–3 lace.
  • Overbite and Curve of Spee for possible reverse-curve needs.

Do

  • If rotations remain, place 18 NT and cinch the terminal ends.
  • If rotations are adequately resolved and progression should be faster, move directly to 16x22 NT.
  • Steel ties on rotations when complete engagement is possible.
  • Maintain anterior 3–3 lace when spacing is present or previously closed.

Document

  • The clinical reason for 18 NT versus skipping to 16x22 NT.
  • Rotations, spacing, and any lace or steel ties.
  • Deep-overbite and Curve-of-Spee findings.
Back to flowchart
Step 04

Into Rectangular NiTi

18 NT → 16x22 NT
Key reminderThis is the major transition from round NiTi to rectangular NiTi. In NiTi with anterior spacing, use a lace from 3–3. Consider lower reverse-curve NiTi for deep overbite leveling.
Written detail

Assess

  • That rotations have improved sufficiently.
  • Overall alignment and treatment timing.
  • Anterior and posterior spacing.
  • Deep overbite and Curve of Spee.
  • The 7s and full wire engagement.

Do

  • Place 16x22 NT.
  • Engage the 7s when possible.
  • Maintain anterior spaces with a 3–3 lace if present.
  • Steel ties on rotated teeth when complete engagement is needed.
  • For deep lower Curve of Spee, use a lower 16x22 NT reverse curve, anterior portion down.
  • Do not place step bends in NiTi.

Document

  • 18 NT → 16x22 NT.
  • Residual rotations, spacing, lace, steel ties, reverse-curve use.
  • Overbite and Curve-of-Spee findings.
Back to flowchart
Step 05

Anterior Space Decision

16x22 NT → 18x25 NT or 16x22 SS
Key reminderAnterior spaces should be closed before continuing through the later NiTi stages. Posterior spaces may remain. Always lace 3–3 in the anterior when placing 18x25 NT.
Written detail

Assess

  • Anterior spaces: size, location, and whether fully closed.
  • Rotations and complete bracket engagement.
  • Treatment timing.
  • Deep overbite and Curve of Spee.
  • Posterior spaces, but do not require them closed at this stage.

Do

  • No meaningful anterior spaces: place 18x25 NT and maintain closure with a 3–3 lace.
  • Anterior spaces remain: place 16x22 SS and use 2–2 or 3–3 power chain to actively close them.
  • With 3–3 power chain, steel-tie canines as indicated.
  • Once closed, progress to 18x25 NT and lace anterior 3–3.
  • For deep lower Curve of Spee, use lower 18x25 NT reverse curve. No step bends in NiTi.

Document

  • 16x22 NT → 18x25 NT, or 16x22 NT → 16x22 SS.
  • Anterior-space status and power-chain location if used.
  • 3–3 lace, rotations, steel ties, bite and Curve-of-Spee findings.
Back to flowchart
Step 06

Large Rectangular NiTi

18x25 NT → 21x25 NT
Key reminderPosterior spaces do not need to be fully closed before moving to 21x25 NT.
Written detail

Assess

  • That anterior spaces are already closed.
  • That closure is being maintained with a 3–3 lace.
  • Rotations and overall alignment.
  • Curve of Spee and overbite.
  • Posterior spaces — document them; they need not be closed yet.
  • That treatment timing is on track.

Do

  • Place 21x25 NT.
  • Continue or replace the anterior 3–3 lace.
  • Engage the 7s and verify complete wire seating.
  • Optional power chain all around, but expect limited closure because of thick NiTi and friction.

Document

  • 18x25 NT → 21x25 NT.
  • Anterior closure, posterior-space status, 3–3 lace, power chain if used.
  • Rotations and bite / Curve-of-Spee findings.
Back to flowchart
Step 07

Reactivate

21x25 NT again
Key reminderA second 21x25 NT appointment is part of the usual progression before stainless steel. It is not a sign that something has gone wrong.
Written detail

Assess

  • Whether alignment and torque expression are continuing to improve.
  • Rotations — should be minimal or resolved.
  • That anterior spaces are closed and stable with the 3–3 lace.
  • Posterior spaces — they may still be present.
  • Curve of Spee and overbite.
  • Wire engagement, brackets, oral hygiene, treatment timing.

Do

  • Reactivate or continue 21x25 NT for another round. Do not change wire size.
  • Maintain the anterior 3–3 lace.
  • Optional power chain all around, recognizing closure is usually limited in thick NiTi.
  • Verify complete engagement and control.

Document

  • That 21x25 NT was reactivated.
  • Anterior closure and posterior spaces.
  • Rotations, 3–3 lace, any power chain, bite and Curve-of-Spee findings.
Back to flowchart
Step 08

Enter Stainless Steel

21x25 NT → 18x25 SS
Key reminderStep bends are done in stainless steel, not in NiTi. Anterior closure should already be established before advancing.
Written detail

Assess

  • Treatment timing and overall alignment.
  • That anterior spaces remain closed and stable.
  • Posterior spaces, and plan closure in stainless steel.
  • Curve of Spee and overbite depth.
  • That rotations are controlled and wire engagement is complete.

Do

  • Place 18x25 SS.
  • Maintain anterior 3–3 lace if needed.
  • Begin or continue posterior space closure as indicated.
  • If deep-overbite leveling is still needed, place step-down bends in the stainless-steel wire.
  • Verify full wire engagement and control.

Document

  • 21x25 NT → 18x25 SS.
  • Anterior and posterior space status.
  • Lace, power chain, and any step-down bends.
  • Bite, Curve of Spee, and finishing findings.
Back to flowchart
Step 09

Finishing Wire

18x25 SS → 19x25 SS
Key reminderSome selected underbite cases may finish in 18x25 SS. 21x25 SS is rare, reserved for additional torque needs.
Written detail

Assess

  • Treatment timing and overall finishing status.
  • Posterior-space closure and interproximal contacts.
  • Torque, residual rotations, and root positioning.
  • Overbite, Curve of Spee, midlines, and overall occlusion.
  • Soft tissue, loose brackets, poking wires, oral hygiene.

Do

  • Place 19x25 SS.
  • Continue remaining posterior space closure as needed.
  • Add detailing bends or finishing adjustments as needed.
  • Continue step-down bends if deep-bite correction remains necessary.
  • Verify complete wire seating and overall control.

Document

  • 18x25 SS → 19x25 SS.
  • Residual spaces, bite, midlines, torque, finishing findings.
  • Elastics, bends, and finishing mechanics used.
  • Complete treatment card and clinical notes.

Example notes

Month 8/24. Rotations improved; no significant anterior spacing. Progressed to lower 16x22 NT reverse curve for deep Curve-of-Spee leveling. 3–3 lace maintained. 7s engaged. Doctor reviewed.
Month 12/24. Anterior spaces closed after 16x22 SS + 3–3 PC. Progressed to 18x25 NT; anterior 3–3 lace placed to maintain closure. Posterior spaces remain and will be addressed later in SS.
Month 15/24. Reactivated 21x25 NT for second round. Anterior closure stable with 3–3 lace. Posterior spaces remain. No significant rotations. Bite improving.
Month 18/24. Progressed 21x25 NT → 18x25 SS. Began posterior space closure. Step-down bends placed for residual deep overbite. Documented finishing mechanics.
