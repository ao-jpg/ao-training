matching name and it appears automatically. Until then the page shows a neutral
"not yet added" panel instead of a broken icon.

Rules: all lowercase, hyphens, no spaces. Photos go in `img/`, videos in `video/`.
Run `compress-videos.ps1` on raw footage before placing it in `video/`.

## Before you shoot
- No patient faces, no names spoken aloud, no chart screens with real PHI visible.
- Typodonts, mannequins, and staff volunteers only.
- For the Dolphin screen recording, use a demo patient record, not a real one.
- Anything that must show a real patient goes on Google Drive with domain-restricted
  sharing, never in the repo. Swap the `<figure>` block for the Drive iframe pattern.
