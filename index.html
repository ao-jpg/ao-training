Clinical Training Manual

Your first two weeks, one day at a time.

Welcome to Alexandria Orthodontics. During your first weeks you are not expected to know how to perform active orthodontic adjustments. You are expected to learn the room, the instruments, the language, and the standards we hold, so that when you do begin working chairside you are building on something solid.

Each day below has its own page. Work through it in order, watch the videos, and use the checklist at the bottom to track what you have covered. At the end of each day, review the checklist with your trainer and have them sign off.

Before you start

Bookmark this page on your phone. Your checkboxes are saved in the browser you use, so use the same device each day if you want your progress to carry over. Days appear here as they are released.

Questions

Anything you are unsure about goes to your trainer, the Clinic Coordinator, or Dr. Lee. There is no such thing as a question that is too basic during your first two weeks. Guessing at chairside is the one thing we do not want.
